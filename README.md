## 📌 Overview
This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** developed using **Power BI** to analyze sales activities, customer behavior, product performance, and sales trends over time.

The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable insights, enabling businesses to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify top-performing products and customers, and evaluate sales trends for better decision-making.

---

## ❗ Problem Statement
Businesses often struggle to monitor sales performance effectively due to fragmented data and lack of interactive reporting systems.

Key challenges include:
- Difficulty tracking overall sales performance  
- Limited visibility into customer purchasing behavior  
- Poor understanding of product contribution to revenue  
- Challenges identifying sales trends over time  
- Inability to quickly monitor KPIs and top customers  

This project addresses these challenges by providing a centralized and interactive dashboard for sales analysis.

## 🔗 Data Source
The dataset used contains sales transaction records, including:

- Product categories  
- Customer information  
- Sales amounts  
- Order quantities  
- Order dates  
- Customer gender data  

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning and Preparation
To ensure accurate analysis, the following data preparation steps were performed:

- Removed duplicate records  
- Handled missing values  
- Standardized product category names  
- Corrected inconsistent formatting  
- Converted sales and quantity fields into numeric data types  
- Structured data for Power BI modeling and visualization  

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
EDA was conducted to uncover trends and patterns within the dataset:

- Analyzed total sales performance  
- Examined product category contributions  
- Evaluated customer gender distribution  
- Identified top-performing customers  
- Analyzed sales trends over time  
- Compared order volume and quantities sold

## 📊 Dashboard Design Summary

### **Dashboard Components**
The dashboard includes:

- **KPI Cards**
  - Total Sales  
  - Number of Orders  
  - Total Quantity Sold  
  - Highest Order Value  

- **Bar Chart**
  - Sales by Product Category  
  - Top 5 Customers  

- **Pie Chart**
  - Customer Gender Distribution  

- **Line Chart**
  - Sales Trend Over Time  

- **Interactive Filters**
  - Product Category  
  - Gender  

---

## 🎨 Colour Theme and Layout
The dashboard uses a clean green-themed design to reflect growth, business performance, and readability.

### **Design Decisions**
- KPI cards positioned at the top for quick performance overview  
- Interactive filters placed on the left for easy navigation  
- Charts arranged logically for smooth analytical flow  
- Consistent color palette used for clarity and professionalism  

---

## ⚙️ Filters and Interactivity
Interactive features implemented include:

- Product category filter  
- Gender filter  
- Cross-filtering between visuals  
- Dynamic KPI updates based on user selections  

These features allow users to explore the dashboard interactively and gain deeper insights.

---

## 📈 Results and Findings

### **Key Insights**
- Total sales reached approximately **$43K**  
- Furniture generated the highest sales among product categories  
- Male customers contributed slightly more to total purchases than female customers  
- A small group of customers accounted for a large share of revenue  
- Sales showed a declining trend over the years analyzed  

---

### **Trend Observations**
- Sales performance decreased gradually over time  
- Product contribution to total sales was uneven  
- Customer purchasing behavior varied significantly  

---

### **Business Implications**
- High-performing product categories should receive more strategic focus  
- Customer retention strategies can target top buyers  
- Declining sales trends may require revised marketing and sales strategies  

---

## ⚠️ Challenges and Solutions

### **Challenges**
- Inconsistent product naming conventions  
- Handling missing transaction values  
- Creating balanced visual layouts for multiple KPIs  

### **Solutions**
- Standardized category names during cleaning  
- Validated numeric fields for accuracy  
- Applied structured dashboard design principles for readability  

---

## 💡 Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following recommendations are suggested:

- Increase investment in high-performing product categories  
- Implement loyalty programs for top customers  
- Investigate causes of declining sales trends  
- Improve marketing efforts toward underperforming categories  
- Use customer segmentation strategies for targeted campaigns
